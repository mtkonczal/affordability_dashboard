# Demo Changes — July 28, 2026

- **Real median income made explicit, national card added (uncommitted):** Confirmed both the national and state median-household-income series arrive already inflation-adjusted (Census's CPI-U-RS series, `MEHOINUSA672N`/`MEHOINUS{ST}A672N`) and correctly show identical Nominal/Real values — then made that legible on the card itself instead of only in the About page: the state card title changed from "Median Household Income" to "Real Median Household Income." Added a "US Median Income" card to the National tab's Work & Wages category — the series already existed and fed the dashed comparison line on state income charts, but was flagged overlay-only and could never be selected on its own. Also confirmed the 20th-percentile income series is genuinely nominal (ACS quintile limits, unlike CPS median income, and correctly deflated by the Real toggle) and labeled it "Income: 20th Percentile (Nominal)" on both the national and state cards for the same at-a-glance clarity.
- **Removed box (33ffe91):** Deleted the "Hours to afford" navy callout from the National view (groceries priced by the week, market rent by the month, both converted to hours at the average wage) along with its component — cut for being a distraction from the core cards rather than for any data problem.
- **Date display fixed (cac7db7):** Renamed the "2019" anchor button to "Pre-COVID" (hovering still shows "December 2019"), and added cadence-aware date formatting so a card states its date at the precision the underlying data actually supports — annual series show just the year, weekly series a full date, monthly/quarterly the existing month-and-year — rather than every series printing a full calendar date regardless of how often it's actually published. Applied to chart tooltips and the Copy-fact sentence. Butter, added earlier the same day, was cut again: its BLS series only starts in April 2018 (the prior series was discontinued in 2012 with no bridge), so it showed a blank line under "Since 2000" and "Max."
- **Second cuts, and dollars instead of index points:** Trimmed eight more national indicators and added four series (plus converted one), taking the national view from 35 cards to 31. Cut three that were misleading or duplicated something already on the page (health insurance CPI, which prices insurer overhead rather than premiums and fell 2% in a year the ACA benchmark premium rose 26%; derived real hourly earnings, which the page-wide Real toggle already produces; the $1.86T national student loan balance, covered per person and per state by the NY Fed series), three labor-market slack series that aren't affordability measures (job openings, quit rate, median weeks unemployed), and two flat grocery items (bananas, potatoes). Mortgage delinquency and new cars were both proposed for the cut and kept. Converted electricity from a CPI index to the BLS average price per kWh and added natural gas per therm — 19.8 cents/kWh, up from 13.3 in 2019, is quotable in a way an index level of 305.7 is not. Added coffee (up 133% since Dec 2019), bacon and butter to groceries. Moved headline CPI out of "Work & wages" into its own "All prices (CPI)" category, since it's the price level everything else is measured against and the deflator behind the Real toggle. Also drafted `grocery_basket_spec.md` — a proposal, not yet built, for summing a fixed eight-item cart into one dollar figure ($29.32 in Dec 2019 → $43.29 today).

- **Missing values (7058dad):** Fixed broken/wrong source metadata for three BLS-only CPI series (car insurance, health insurance, childcare) that aren't carried on FRED, corrected the health insurance series to pull the right BLS item code (SEME instead of the medicinal-drugs code SEMF), and made "view source" links for derived metrics (like Rent in Hours of Work) open the in-app About view instead of the bare about.html page.
- **First cuts (91a3db0):** Trimmed six redundant or lower-value indicators (CPI rent, mean weeks unemployed, income 80th percentile, revolving credit, auto loans, consumer loan delinquency) from both the national and state data, keeping the clearer or more affordability-relevant series in each pair.
- **Test cases (7871d60):** Added a full Node-based test suite (data contract + helper-logic tests), GitHub Actions workflows for running tests and updating data, and expanded CLAUDE.md/README documentation.
- **About the data cleaned up (9145e65):** Restyled the About page — removed section-number tags and the callout "method" boxes, widened paragraphs to match table width, and switched cadence badges to muted bordered pills consistent with the rest of the tracker's UI.
